FIG CLease safety near the drift boundary

Share of trials with a linearizability violation (Porcupine Illegal or a provably stale read), with Wilson 95% intervals. The 0.92× point is the new 60-trial run. The normal and 0.25× points come from the 3-trial pilot and are shown only as reference points; they are not pooled with the 60 trials.

Condition (lease read path)SourceTrialsViolationsRateWilson 95%

At 0.92× the lease lasts about 0.978 s of real time against a 1 s minimum election timeout, a margin of about 2%. Zero violations in 60 trials bounds the per-trial violation rate below 6.0% at 95% confidence. It does not prove the configuration safe. The lease path is safe only while clock drift stays within the configured bound; the 0.25× pilot shows what happens when it does not.
